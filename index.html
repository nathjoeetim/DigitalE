Who We Are

About Digital Equity Africa

We are a digital inclusion and transformation consultancy focused on closing the digital divide across Africa through practical, scalable, and inclusive innovation.

We work at the intersection of technology, communication, education, and policy to help governments, development partners, corporations, and learning communities navigate digital transformation, amplify impact, and build future-ready ecosystems.

At Digital Equity Africa, our mission is to advance digital inclusion by designing and delivering transformative programs, advisory services, and innovation platforms that equip individuals, institutions, and governments with the skills and systems needed to thrive in a digital-first economy.

We bridge the digital divide by building scalable learning ecosystems, strengthening digital capabilities, and enabling meaningful participation in the global technology landscape.

To be Africa’s leading catalyst for inclusive digital transformation—where every individual, regardless of background, has the opportunity to access, shape, and benefit from the digital economy.

We envision a continent where inclusive-by-design innovation drives shared prosperity, reduces inequality, and unlocks Africa’s full digital potential.

What We Deliver

Our Services & Solutions

💻

1. Corporate Digitization

We help organizations move from traditional systems to digitally enabled, future-ready operations. Our focus is not just technology adoption but building smarter workflows, empowered teams, and scalable digital infrastructure.

What We Deliver
  • Digital transformation strategy & advisory
  • Process digitization & workflow optimization
  • Workforce digital capability building
  • Systems integration & operational redesign
  • Change management & adoption support
Who This Is For

Government agencies, Corporate organizations, Development institutions, SMEs scaling operations.

Expected Outcome Improved efficiency, reduced operational bottlenecks, stronger digital capability across teams, and future-ready institutions.
🤖

2. AI Immersions

We design immersive AI learning and exposure experiences that help leaders, teams, and institutions understand how artificial intelligence is reshaping industries, translating global innovation into practical local application.

What We Deliver
  • Executive AI immersion programs
  • Government & institutional AI briefings
  • Sector-specific AI workshops
  • Innovation lab experiences & simulations
  • Applied AI strategy sessions
  • Kids & youth AI immersion camps
Who This Is For

Government leaders, policymakers, Corporate executives, Innovation teams, Development agencies, Universities.

Expected Outcome Clear understanding of AI opportunities, practical use cases for your sector, and actionable pathways for adoption.
📚

3. AI Literacy & Future-Readiness

We prepare children, young people, and professionals for the future of work through structured AI literacy and digital skills programs, blending creativity, problem-solving, and real-world application.

What We Deliver
  • AI literacy programs for children and youth
  • Future skills training (digital, creative, innovation)
  • AI summer camps & bootcamps
  • Future Minds learning community programs
  • School & tech education partnerships
Who This Is For

Schools, educational institutions, Parents, guardians, Youth organizations, Governments & ministries.

Expected Outcome Learners who are confident with technology, equipped with AI knowledge, and prepared for future careers in a digital economy.
🎉

4. Kids Tech Fest AI Summit

Our flagship initiative introducing children to technology, creativity, and AI through fun, interactive, hands-on learning experiences. Gathered over 5,000 attendees in Lagos during its maiden edition in 2025.

What We Deliver
  • Hands-on coding, hardware, and AI workshops
  • Interactive tech exhibitions & playground
  • Creative panels for teachers, parents & children
  • Innovation pitch competitions & showcases
Who This Is For

Children (ages 5-18), parents, teachers, educational institutions, sponsors, ecosystem partners.

Expected Outcome Moving children from passive users of technology to confident creators, problem-solvers, and future innovators.
📢

5. PR & Strategic Communications

We help organizations, governments, and ecosystem players shape powerful narratives, build visibility, and communicate impact in ways that attract partnerships, funding, and influence.

What We Deliver
  • Strategic communications advisory
  • Thought leadership positioning
  • Media relations & visibility campaigns
  • Stakeholder engagement strategy
  • Narrative development for programs
Who This Is For

Government institutions, Development agencies, Tech startups, NGOs, Executive profiles.

Expected Outcome Stronger visibility, clearer messaging, improved stakeholder trust, and increased influence in the African tech sector.
🏛️

6. Tech Conferences & Event Management

We conceptualize, design, and execute high-impact technology conferences and ecosystem events that drive dialogue, visibility, and ecosystem growth across the continent.

What We Deliver
  • Full conference design & project management
  • Agenda & content curation
  • Speaker sourcing & onboarding
  • Production & on-ground execution
  • Audience mobilization & sponsor engagement
Who This Is For

Government agencies, Development organizations, Corporates and sponsors, Tech ecosystems & hubs.

Expected Outcome World-class events that drive visibility, partnerships, ecosystem growth, and policy or industry impact.
